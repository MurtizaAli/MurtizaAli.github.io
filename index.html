About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Engineer at South East Technological University (SETU), Carlow, Ireland, where I develop signal processing and machine learning algorithms for FMCW radar, with a focus on contactless respiration and heart-rate monitoring in multi-person environments. In November 2026 I will join the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) in Copenhagen as a Postdoctoral Researcher in neuromorphic acoustics.

I completed my Ph.D. in Signal Processing at IIT Jammu as a Prime Minister's Research Fellow. My doctoral work addressed robust direction-of-arrival (DoA) estimation for underwater and aerial acoustic sources using sparse recovery and deep learning. Originally from the Kashmir Valley, I also have hands-on experience designing and deploying deep neural networks on embedded platforms for real-time applications.
